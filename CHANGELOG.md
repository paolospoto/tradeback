## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added

- Trade evolutions for generations I to V: Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver, Crystal, Ruby, Sapphire,
  Emerald, FireRed, LeafGreen, Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, SoulSilver, Black, White, Black 2
  and White 2.
- Party and PC box Pokémon, with held item requirements, the Everstone, nickname, ability, stats, HP
  and Pokédex updates.
- Checks before editing (checksums, encryption round trip, party stats) and after editing (checksums,
  no changes outside the evolved Pokémon and the Pokédex).
- Raw `.sav` and `.dsv` files, DeSmuME footers and emulator clock bytes.
- Step-by-step help for exporting and importing saves with Delta.
